# Deskripsi gambar: struktur data center bawah danau

## Bahasa Indonesia

Gambar ini menyajikan rancangan konseptual data center bawah danau berbentuk limas persegi terpancung, yang diuraikan melalui tampak depan, potongan A-A, denah rangka atap, dan detail sambungan kedap. Wadah direncanakan beroperasi terendam di kolom air danau, dengan dimensi alas 1.800 × 1.800 mm, atap 1.400 × 1.400 mm, dan tinggi badan 1.000 mm. Skid berukuran 2.000 × 2.000 mm menopang struktur, sedangkan tinggi keseluruhan hingga titik angkat atas adalah 1.300 mm, tanpa rigging. Cangkang baja tahan karat (01), pengaku struktural (02), dan lapisan antikorosi (03) membentuk sistem pelindung, sementara skid ballast (04) menyediakan tempat pemberat dan antarmuka penopang. Empat padeye atas (05) meneruskan beban pengangkatan ke rangka pemikul, sehingga tutup servis (06) tidak menjadi jalur beban utama. Tutup tersebut dilengkapi dua seal dengan ruang antar-seal yang dipantau (07). Untuk panjang sisi alas $b = 1{,}8\,\mathrm{m}$, panjang sisi atap $a = 1{,}4\,\mathrm{m}$, dan tinggi $H = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$, volume luar badan adalah $V = H(b^2 + ab + a^2)/3 = 2{,}573\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Dengan asumsi air tawar bermassa jenis $\rho = 1.000\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ dan $g = 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, gaya apung badan tertutup yang terendam penuh mencapai $F_B = \rho gV \approx 25{,}24\,\mathrm{kN}$, belum termasuk perpindahan air oleh komponen eksternal. Pada kedalaman ilustratif $h = 30\,\mathrm{m}$, selisih tekanan terhadap interior yang dipertahankan mendekati tekanan permukaan adalah $\Delta p = \rho gh \approx 294\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Nilai ini merupakan masukan pembebanan, bukan penetapan kedalaman aman; pemilihan material, termasuk kandidat 316L, ketebalan pelat, pengaku, ballast, dan angkur masih memerlukan evaluasi korosi, tekuk akibat tekanan eksternal, serta stabilitas terhadap geser dan guling.

Subsistem komputasi terdiri atas tiga server 2U dengan penyimpanan SSD/NVMe internal yang ditempatkan dalam kaset 8U (08), dengan sisa ruang untuk switch dan pengelolaan kabel. Atmosfer nitrogen (09) diusulkan dengan kelembapan terkendali dan persetujuan pabrikan, sedangkan penetrator daya A/B dan serat optik (10) menghubungkan modul dengan infrastruktur darat. Panas dipindahkan dari gas internal melalui coil gas-cairan dan kipas (14), kemudian melalui sirkuit coolant tertutup (12), pompa A/B (11), dan penukar panas eksternal A/B (13), dengan katup isolasi (18) untuk pemisahan cabang. Konfigurasi ini memisahkan coolant internal dari air danau; keberadaan dua cabang belum membuktikan redundansi kapasitas tanpa pengujian kondisi kegagalan. Dengan asumsi batas daya masukan 600 W per server dan beban bantu 300 W, kebutuhan daya operasi adalah $P = 3(600) + 300 = 2.100\,\mathrm{W}$. Untuk anggaran pembuangan panas $\dot Q = 2{,}5\,\mathrm{kW}$, coolant yang didekati sebagai air dengan $c_p = 4{,}18\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, dan kenaikan suhu $\Delta T = 5\,\mathrm{K}$, neraca energi $\dot m = \dot Q/(c_p\Delta T)$ menghasilkan kebutuhan aliran sekitar $0{,}120\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$ atau $7{,}18\,\mathrm{L\,min^{-1}}$. UPS dengan baterai LiFePO₄ opsional (19) dialokasikan pada ruang bawah yang dipisahkan dari perangkat komputasi (20); target cadangan sepuluh menit pada beban 2,1 kW memerlukan energi keluaran AC $E = Pt = 0{,}35\,\mathrm{kWh}$, sebelum memperhitungkan rugi konversi, batas pengosongan, dan degradasi baterai. Seluruh nilai tersebut merupakan asumsi desain, bukan hasil pengukuran. Sebagai batas kompatibilitas, [server acuan](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/hyper/2u/sys-221h-tnr) mensyaratkan suhu operasi 10-35 °C dan kelembapan relatif 8-80% tanpa kondensasi; dengan demikian, atmosfer nitrogen yang sangat kering tidak dapat langsung dianggap memenuhi spesifikasi. Kinerja termal dan keselamatan baterai tetap memerlukan evaluasi temperatur danau, fouling, kehilangan tekanan aliran, serta pelepasan panas dan gas pada kondisi gangguan.

Ketahanan mekanis perangkat diupayakan melalui empat isolator captive dan snubber berperedam (21) yang menopang kaset server bersama coil pendingin, disertai sambungan fleksibel dan bonding listrik (22), serta pengunci transportasi dan servis (23). Penempatan coil pada rangka bergerak yang sama membatasi perubahan jarak relatif terhadap server, sementara sambungan fleksibel mengakomodasi perpindahan terhadap rangka tetap. Dalam pendekatan linier satu derajat kebebasan, frekuensi alami memenuhi $f_n = (2\pi)^{-1}\sqrt{K/m}$; untuk massa bergerak asumsi $m = 180\,\mathrm{kg}$ dan frekuensi alami percobaan $f_n = 3\,\mathrm{Hz}$, kekakuan total ekuivalen adalah $K \approx 64\,\mathrm{kN\,m^{-1}}$. Perhitungan ini tidak menentukan kapasitas gempa karena respons aktual dipengaruhi redaman, resonansi, gerak rotasional, pembagian beban, dan kontak dengan snubber, sebagaimana ditekankan dalam [panduan rekayasa seismik](https://mason-ind.com/seismic-engineering-svcs/). Pemeriksaan geometris pendahuluan mengalokasikan translasi pusat rakitan sebesar ±25 mm pada arah horizontal, ±10 mm pada arah vertikal, dan rotasi relatif ±1° pada setiap sumbu; alokasi tersebut hanya mencakup kaset dan coil, sehingga tidak merepresentasikan stroke isolator yang telah dikualifikasi ataupun kemampuan menjaga rak tetap horizontal. Sensor temperatur, kelembapan, oksigen, tekanan, dan gerak (15), kamera dengan jendela serta wiper (16), dan tray pendeteksi kebocoran (17) menyediakan masukan bagi distribusi daya dan kontrol keselamatan (24). Pemeliharaan direncanakan setelah unit diangkat ke permukaan, melalui bukaan atas bersih 1.000 × 1.200 mm. Oleh karena itu, gambar ini mendefinisikan arsitektur komponen dan dasar perhitungan awal, sedangkan klaim kedap, efisiensi, kedalaman operasi, kapasitas angkat, dan ketahanan gempa harus ditetapkan melalui analisis struktur, karakterisasi lokasi, serta pengujian rakitan terintegrasi.

## English

The figure presents a conceptual underlake data center configured as a truncated square pyramid, illustrated through a front elevation, section A-A, roof-framing plan, and watertight joint detail. The enclosure is intended to operate submerged in the lake water column, with a base measuring 1,800 × 1,800 mm, a roof measuring 1,400 × 1,400 mm, and a body height of 1,000 mm. A 2,000 × 2,000 mm skid supports the structure, while the overall height to the upper lifting points is 1,300 mm, excluding rigging. The stainless-steel shell (01), structural stiffeners (02), and corrosion-protection coating (03) constitute the protective system, whereas the ballast skid (04) accommodates ballast and provides the support interface. Four upper padeyes (05) transfer lifting loads to the load-bearing frame, preventing the service lid (06) from serving as the principal lifting load path. The lid incorporates two seals separated by a monitored interspace (07). For a base side length $b = 1.8\,\mathrm{m}$, roof side length $a = 1.4\,\mathrm{m}$, and height $H = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, the external body volume is $V = H(b^2 + ab + a^2)/3 = 2.573\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Assuming freshwater density $\rho = 1{,}000\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and gravitational acceleration $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, the fully submerged, sealed body experiences a buoyant force of $F_B = \rho gV \approx 25.24\,\mathrm{kN}$, excluding displacement by external components. At an illustrative depth $h = 30\,\mathrm{m}$, the pressure differential relative to an interior maintained near surface pressure is $\Delta p = \rho gh \approx 294\,\mathrm{kPa}$. This value defines a loading input rather than an allowable operating depth; material selection, including the candidate grade 316L, plate thickness, stiffeners, ballast, and anchorage remain subject to corrosion assessment, external-pressure buckling analysis, and verification of sliding and overturning stability.

The computing subsystem comprises three 2U servers with internal SSD/NVMe storage housed in an 8U cassette (08), with the remaining space allocated to a network switch and cable management. A nitrogen atmosphere (09) is proposed subject to controlled humidity and manufacturer approval, while A/B power and fibre-optic feedthroughs (10) connect the module to shore infrastructure. Heat is transferred from the internal gas through a gas-to-liquid coil and fans (14), followed by a closed coolant circuit (12), A/B pumps (11), and A/B external heat exchangers (13), with isolation valves (18) enabling branch separation. This configuration separates the internal coolant from lake water; the presence of two branches does not establish redundant capacity without testing under failure conditions. Assuming an input-power cap of 600 W per server and an auxiliary load of 300 W, the operating power requirement is $P = 3(600) + 300 = 2{,}100\,\mathrm{W}$. For a heat-rejection budget $\dot Q = 2.5\,\mathrm{kW}$, coolant approximated as water with $c_p = 4.18\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, and a temperature rise $\Delta T = 5\,\mathrm{K}$, the energy balance $\dot m = \dot Q/(c_p\Delta T)$ yields a required flow of approximately $0.120\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$ or $7.18\,\mathrm{L\,min^{-1}}$. An optional UPS with a LiFePO₄ battery (19) is allocated to a lower bay separated from the computing equipment (20); a ten-minute backup target at 2.1 kW requires an AC output energy of $E = Pt = 0.35\,\mathrm{kWh}$, before accounting for conversion losses, discharge limits, and battery degradation. These values are design assumptions rather than measurements. As a compatibility constraint, the [reference server](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/hyper/2u/sys-221h-tnr) specifies an operating temperature of 10-35 °C and non-condensing relative humidity of 8-80%; consequently, a very dry nitrogen atmosphere cannot be assumed to satisfy its environmental specification. Thermal performance and battery safety still require assessment of lake temperature, fouling, flow pressure losses, and heat and gas release under fault conditions.

Mechanical protection of the equipment is addressed through four captive isolators and cushioned snubbers (21) supporting the server cassette and cooling coil, together with flexible connections and electrical bonding (22), and transport and service locks (23). Mounting the coil on the same moving frame limits changes in its position relative to the servers, while flexible connections accommodate displacement relative to the fixed frame. Under a linear single-degree-of-freedom approximation, the natural frequency follows $f_n = (2\pi)^{-1}\sqrt{K/m}$; for an assumed moving mass $m = 180\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a trial natural frequency $f_n = 3\,\mathrm{Hz}$, the equivalent total stiffness is $K \approx 64\,\mathrm{kN\,m^{-1}}$. This calculation does not establish seismic capacity because the actual response depends on damping, resonance, rotational motion, load distribution, and snubber contact, as emphasised in [seismic engineering guidance](https://mason-ind.com/seismic-engineering-svcs/). A preliminary geometric assessment reserves assembly-centre translations of ±25 mm horizontally and ±10 mm vertically, together with relative rotations of ±1° about each axis; this allocation covers only the cassette and coil and therefore does not represent qualified isolator stroke or the ability to maintain a level rack. Temperature, humidity, oxygen, pressure, and motion sensors (15), a camera with a window and wiper (16), and a leak-detection tray (17) provide inputs to power distribution and safety controls (24). Maintenance is planned after recovery to the surface, using a clear top opening of 1,000 × 1,200 mm. Accordingly, the figure defines the component architecture and preliminary calculation basis, whereas claims concerning watertightness, efficiency, operating depth, lifting capacity, and seismic resistance must be established through structural analysis, site characterisation, and integrated assembly testing.
